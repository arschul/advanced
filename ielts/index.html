IELTS Listening

Full practice tests in the IELTS Listening format: four parts, forty questions, read aloud by your browser’s own voices. Play a whole test or one part at a time, and finish with a percentage score.

How to use these. Put the test on the projector and press Play. The recording includes the instructions and the reading time before each set of questions, just like the real exam. Students can answer on screen or on the printed question paper. Check answers gives a score for whatever you played: one part out of ten, or the full test out of forty. Turn on Test conditions to play each part once only, with no skipping. Use Voices to choose a different voice for any speaker; Edge’s “Natural” voices sound the most realistic.
