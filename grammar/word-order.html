Word order

English word order is strict. Frequency adverbs go before the main verb but after be (She always walks. She is always late.); the object comes before the adverb (She speaks English well); enough follows adjectives but comes before nouns; adjectives follow a fixed order; and a question inside a sentence uses statement order: Do you know where the station is?
