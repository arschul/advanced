Grammar Practice

Thirty sessions on the grammar points the TOEFL ITP tests most, in eight themes. Each session explains one point, drills it in the exact Structure and Written Expression format, then practices it in other ways.

How a session runs

Seven blocks and 100 items. Use the whole session or any block on its own. Each session prints as a worksheet and as a separate answer key.

Warm-up6 itemsExplainThe patternsStructure30 itemsWritten Expression30 itemsPractice16 itemsProduction10 itemsExit check8 items

Beyond the TOEFL format. Practice and Production rotate through seven activity types: sorting into categories, matching, unscrambling sentences, typed gap-fill, finding every error in a paragraph, combining two sentences, and rewriting a sentence from a given start.
