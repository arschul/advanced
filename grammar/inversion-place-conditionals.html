Inversion with place and conditionals

Two more kinds of inversion appear on the TOEFL. After a place expression at the start, the verb can come before the subject: On the hill stands an old church. And in formal conditionals, if disappears and the helping verb moves to the front: Had I known …, Were it not for …, Should you need …
