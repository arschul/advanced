Countable and uncountable nouns

Countable nouns have a plural and take many, few, a few and a / an: many books, a few chairs. Uncountable nouns have no plural and take much, little, a little: much information, a little advice. Some words that are countable in other languages — advice, information, furniture, homework — are uncountable in English.
