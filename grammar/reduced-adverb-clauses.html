Reduced adverb clauses

When both clauses have the same subject, an adverb clause can lose its subject and its be: While I was walking home, I saw an accident becomes While walking home, I saw an accident. The subject of the main clause must be the one doing (or receiving) the action, or the sentence says something absurd.
